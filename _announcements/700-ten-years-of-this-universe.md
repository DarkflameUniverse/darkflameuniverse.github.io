Hello Explorers,

On October 26th, it'll mark 10 years since the general public got to play LEGO Universe. While the game might've only lasted till early 2012, we've been hard at work for years to recreate it and give it more time to shine.

### Crash Testing: Final Content Update

Today we opened up our Crash Test servers again and let our testers experience Nimbus Station, some even getting to visit it for the first time. Testers were able to join a faction and earn faction tokens to buy new gear, with some even hitting Rank 2 in the first hours of testing. The test didn't stop there, however; we retrofitted Avant Gardens Survival and Battle of Nimbus Station to be two event-specific instances.

Avant Gardens Survival became an all-out PvP instance, where players could go to test their new faction gear out against other new recruits.

Battle of Nimbus Station serves as a proving ground for new recruits against Maelstrom from across the universe, including named enemies from Crux Prime.

These aren't permanent changes, as both instances will go back to their original state when they launch in the future. It's simply a temporary event to celebrate LEGO Universe's birthday and to serve as a test for what we can do in the future. Private/special instances and joining them is a new feature Wincent, one of our developers, added just before this event which made it all possible.

### Closed Beta

While we're still working through all the details on the testing phase, we can share what our goals for the content included will be:

- Gnarled Forest
- Properties
- Spider Queen
- and all other Crash Test content!

We'll be accepting a lot more people into Closed Beta and in waves to see how our server handles it. If you didn't get a key for the Closed Alpha, you'll have a much better shot to get one here! At a later date, you'll be able to directly apply for Beta access via the My Account page on our website. Don't get in that way? Various giveaways will occur on our Twitter, on our forum, in livestreams or even in our Discord that'll be opened up to the public.
